WHO WE ARE

More Than Just a Water Bottle

AquaPure is a reusable water bottle brand focused on everyday convenience, modern designs and a more responsible way to stay hydrated.

From stainless steel bottles to colourful everyday designs, our collection is made for students, workers, travellers and active people.

We want to make reusable bottles an easy choice for everyone. A small change in your daily routine can be a positive step towards reducing single-use plastic waste.
